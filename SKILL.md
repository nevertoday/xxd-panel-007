# XXD Panel 007 · 手写生活观察图谱

Turn each supplied photograph into a finished handwritten observation atlas. Preserve the real photograph only in paired modes; every transformed frame must decompose the same source into several small physical-looking views—whole pieces, close-ups, sections, repeats, or detail fragments—loosely spread through the upper and middle paper field. Thin black handwritten notes weave between them so the eye alternates between looking and reading. The result feels like an intimate record of material, touch, taste, sound, action, or memory supported by the source, never a rigid infographic or generic sticker sheet.

Operational rules follow the shared XXD Panel workflow contract: four combinable modes; source-adaptive ordinary canvases; exact 50/50 paired geometry; linked or independent four-device wallpapers; copy and locale preflight; fresh generation jobs; privacy-preserving raster generation; and one fresh task directory per source and mode. Style-specific sections may refine aesthetics, copy hierarchy, and acceptance criteria but never override this contract.

## Non-negotiable contract

- One source photo may be processed in one or more selected modes. Each selected ordinary mode (`top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`) produces one PNG; selected `wallpaper-pack` produces exactly four separate PNGs. Selecting all four modes therefore produces seven final files per source. Keep every mode in its own task directory and never combine modes, sources, or wallpapers into a grid, contact sheet, or overview.
- Resolve a non-empty ordered set of modes before generation: `top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`, and/or `wallpaper-pack`. Accept one choice, multiple choices separated by `+`, Chinese/English commas or whitespace, natural-language names, or `全部` / `all`. Deduplicate repeated choices and execute in menu order 1→4. If none is specified, ask with the multiline multi-select menu in the workflow. Do not ask again when the set is already clear.
- When the selected set contains `wallpaper-pack`, require a second choice: `linked` or `independent`. A linked pack approves one anchor wallpaper, iPad by default, and makes every other device reference both the original photo and that same anchor. An independent pack gives every device only the original photo. Neither permits resizing or cropping one wallpaper into the others. Do not ask this follow-up when wallpaper mode is not selected.
- Paired modes split exactly 50/50. `design-only` and every wallpaper contain no visible source photograph, no seam, and no reserved photographic panel.
- Exact user pixels have highest priority, followed by an explicit ratio or destination. Otherwise ordinary modes adapt to the complete source: `top-bottom` = `W×2H`, `left-right` = `2W×H`, and `design-only` = `W×H`. Never impose the archived initial brief's 3:4 canvas as a silent default.
- Visible photography remains faithful. Allow only restrained editorial grading and environmental extension needed for an explicitly overridden canvas. Never stretch, distort, repaint, replace, or structurally alter the subject.
- The transformed frame must preserve identity, material, action, function, and visible relation through at least three source-specific cues distributed across several observation fragments. Clean white paper remains dominant; small rounded object-like drawings keep source colour, material, slight perspective, and hand-painted evidence; thin black handwritten copy becomes the reading backbone. It is not a rigid grid, icon set, generic sticker sheet, dense infographic, commercial cartoon, glossy vector art, photoreal painting, or 3D.
- Copy has no silent default. Resolve automatic copy, exact custom copy, or text-free output before image generation. Automatic and custom modes also require a target language or locale. By default, one source-specific copy package is shared verbatim across all selected modes; explicit per-mode copy instructions override it.
- Render no logo, watermark, signature, colour swatch, UI, device mockup, decorative pseudo-text, or unrelated explanatory prose.

## Aesthetic motive lock

Modes and device ratios may change placement and aspect ratio but never the 007 motive. Every transformed frame must visibly express this chain:

**this exact photographed subject or inseparable relation → preserve at least three identity, material, action, function, or relation cues → decompose it into several small physical-looking whole views, close-ups, section cuts, repeats, or detail fragments → retain rounded contours, slight perspective, source colour, material texture, and hand-painted evidence → distribute the fragments loosely through the upper and middle of a dominant clean-white paper field → build a staggered, near-horizontal reading rhythm rather than a table → thread thin black handwritten short words, phrases, sound cues, and annotations around and between the drawings → finish with scanned-paper softness, pigment absorption, uneven edges, and a small optional footer line**.

The intimacy comes from source-specific observation: what a surface might feel like, how a part repeats, where material breaks, which action makes a sound, or which visible detail carries memory. These readings must be supported by the image or user context, not invented as biography. Handwritten observation pages, picture-book information spreads, art publishing, and relaxed editorial design are construction influences rather than templates.

Reject the result as generic when an unrelated photo could replace the source without materially changing the selected fragments, material cues, handwritten observations, or reading order. Also reject evenly sized icons, strict rows or columns, repeated card containers, stock arrows, generic labels, decorative pseudo-text, dense coverage, heavy drop shadows, borders, polished modernist typography, commercial-cartoon styling, photoreal shading, 3D volume, and overdesign.

## 007 visual system

### Several source-specific observation fragments

- Privately lock at least three source-specific cues across contour, pose/orientation, function, proportion, material, colour, opening, connection, overlap, negative shape, or relationship distance.
- Decompose the principal subject or inseparable relation into several useful views without imposing a fixed count: one recognisable whole or main fragment, plus only the close-ups, sections, repeats, small pieces, or detail crops needed to reveal source-specific material, action, function, or relation.
- People keep pose, direction, clothing mass, and relational distance; animals keep body rhythm and head direction; plants keep growth gesture; architecture keeps skyline and defining openings; objects and vehicles keep functional silhouette and negative shape; landscapes keep one source-specific terrain or spatial relation.
- Keep one principal subject or inseparable relationship. Every fragment must belong to it; never add unrelated collectible objects merely to fill the page.

### Loose paper-field reading rhythm

- Let clean white paper occupy the largest area. Spread small fragments mainly through the upper and middle zones, preserving generous staggered gaps and an approximate left-to-right or script-appropriate reading flow.
- Avoid strict tables, equal cells, cards, labelled columns, or a centred specimen grid. Scale, interval, and alignment may vary gently according to the source and copy.
- Use proximity, whitespace, repeated material, and handwritten phrases to connect observations. Arrows or leader lines appear only when truly needed and remain hand-drawn and sparse.
- The lower area may hold one small signature-like line, title, number, date, or theme phrase only when grounded. It must not become a dense footer or branding strip.

### Physical-looking hand-painted miniatures

- Keep every miniature small, rounded, slightly perspectival, and physically specific. Preserve source material, colour, contour, openings, layers, connections, and useful irregularities.
- Show grain, browned edge, crumb, fibre, peel, seam, reflection, texture, or section detail only when the source supports it. The fragments are observations, not universal food icons or kawaii stickers.
- Use hand-painted colour with slight absorption and natural edge variation. Allow gentle tonal compression and small dark or saturated accents, but no thick cast shadow or glossy product rendering.
- Preserve scanned-paper softness, slight compression, uneven edges, and pigment absorption. Reject smooth vector icons, identical outline weight, plastic surfaces, heavy modelling, and 3D.

### White paper, black notes, warm source colour

- White paper occupies the largest area. Thin black handwriting carries information. Subject colours remain warm, clean, slightly high-value, and traceable to the source.
- Use only small high-saturation warm, dark, or theme-supporting accents for focus, shadow, texture, and rhythm.
- Preserve material distinctions without turning every fragment into a different palette. Repeated pieces from one object should still feel related.
- Avoid rainbow sticker palettes, muddy mixing, neon glow, corporate brand systems, uniform beige filtering, and strong modern colour blocking.

## Copy and typography belong to 007

007 text is the observation atlas's structural backbone: thin black handwritten words, phrases, sound cues, and notes weave around the drawings—not a detached caption system, headline wall, UI label, or data display.

- Automatic copy derives a restrained set of short source-bound observations from visible material, action, function, relation, supported sensory suggestion, or known context. Use only as many as the fragments genuinely need.
- Mix brief object words, tactile or action phrases, onomatopoeia, and tiny annotations when natural. A number, date, place, or signature-like footer appears only when supplied or reliably established.
- Preserve exact user wording verbatim. Refine an editable direction only within permission while protecting audience, mandatory terms, tone, and semantic phrase breaks.
- Apply the unrelated-image swap test: generic lifestyle words that survive on another source must be rewritten.

Typography has its own 007 construction:

- **Role:** a reading path that alternates looking and reading and gently connects fragments.
- **Voice:** intimate, observant, fresh, warm, lightly childlike, lively, and readable.
- **Construction:** thin black handwritten short words and sentences with relaxed spacing and a gently jumping baseline; use one smaller footer line only when useful. Do not force one handwriting model across scripts.
- **Placement:** weave notes to the left and right of drawings; let some touch a fragment edge and others float in whitespace. Preserve breathable intervals and native reading direction.
- **Material:** absorbed black ink or lightly softened scan-like handwriting, never glossy type, heavy bold sans, strict typesetting, or pseudo-handwriting repeated as a font pattern.
- **Script:** use a native hand-made editorial equivalent for the resolved locale. Preserve natural Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, and Latin construction rather than imitating Latin handwriting across every script.

Resolve locale independently from command language:

```text
target market or audience > requested output language > direction language; if none is explicit, ask before generation
```

Localise by transcreation using native wording, rhetoric, punctuation, spacing, shaping, and semantic line breaks. Never infer nationality or audience language from appearance, clothing, scenery, filenames, metadata, or signs.

```bash

  --out result.png --layout top-bottom

  --out result.png --layout left-right
```
